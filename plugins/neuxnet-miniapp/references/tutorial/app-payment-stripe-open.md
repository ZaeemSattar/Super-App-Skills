##  Apply to activate Stripe

-   [Login/Register](https://dashboard.stripe.com/login)
    
-   After entering the home page, click the Continue button at the top ![](https://native-res.dcloud.net.cn/images/uniapp/payment/stripe_home_page.png)
    
-   Improve company information ![](https://native-res.dcloud.net.cn/images/uniapp/payment/stripe_company_info.png)
    
-   After completing the information, go back to the homepage to view the key on the right ![](https://native-res.dcloud.net.cn/images/uniapp/payment/stripe_get_publishkey.png)
