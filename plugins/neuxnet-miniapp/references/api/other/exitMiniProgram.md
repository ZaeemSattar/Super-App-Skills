###  uni.exitMiniProgram()3.0.0-3090920240624001

Exit the Mini App

> Equivalent to clicking the close button of the capsule, the Mini App will be hidden in the background and will not be destroyed

**Platform Difference Description**

| Mini App | H5 |
| --- | --- |
| √ | x |

> It takes effect immediately after execution. No parameter is required

**Example**

```

uni.exitMiniProgram()

```
